## 📌 Milestone Overview
This repository contains the strategic research plan, data source identification framework, methodology formulation, and 30–35 hour resource allocation schedule for analyzing publicly available agricultural datasets.
---
## 📑 Core Components of the Plan
### 1. Research Objectives & Key Questions
- **Objective:** Establish a systematic workflow for retrieving, cleaning, and analyzing agricultural data to support crop yield prediction and agribusiness market decisions.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. What historical crop yield patterns exist across regional weather and soil variations?
  2. How do environmental parameters (rainfall, temperature, soil pH) correlate with production outcomes?
  3. Which public data sources provide reliable, multi-seasonal agricultural metrics?
---
### 2. Identified Data Sources
- **Governmental & Official Records:** National/State agricultural databases (e.g., crop yield, production area, historical prices).
- **Environmental & Climate Data:** Open weather APIs and climate history repositories (temperature, precipitation, humidity).
- **Soil Characteristics Data:** Regional soil profiles, nutrient indices (NPK), and pH levels.
---
### 3. Proposed Methodology
1. **Data Extraction & Ingestion:** Scraping and downloading open-access agricultural datasets.
2. **Preprocessing & Cleaning:** Handling missing values, standardizing units, and removing duplicate records.
3. **Exploratory Analysis Plan:** Univariate distributions, trend analysis, and variable correlation matrix.
4. **Model Readiness Setup:** Structuring variables into feature matrices suitable for regression algorithms.

### 5. Risk Assessment & Mitigation
- **Challenge:** Data inconsistency or missing historical records.  
  *Mitigation:* Apply robust interpolation techniques or cross-reference secondary public databases.
- **Challenge:** Differences in measurement units across regions.  
  *Mitigation:* Build a standardized normalization script during preprocessing.
